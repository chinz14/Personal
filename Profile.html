Consumer Audio · Retention & Discovery

Spotify Clip Recall: Turning Saved Podcast Snippets into Scannable Knowledge

0 to 1 User Research PRD Prototype

Spotify shipped Clips in May 2026 but only built the capture half. A saved clip is passive audio with no index or transcript, so retrieving a single key quote forces users to replay minutes of audio. Users abandon the feature within days.

Research & Validation

  • Synthesized 5 sources of public evidence, including a Spotify Community thread with 1,165+ upvotes running since 2015.
  • Conducted a 14-day personal usage trial: 23 clips saved across 8 episodes, confirming zero frictionless repeat visits.
  • Structured problem using Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD) and scoped MVP priorities via RICE confidence scoring.

Shipped Artefacts

  • 9-slide executive product deck unpacking user behavior and unit economics.
  • Comprehensive PRD: 7 product requirements, 6 edge cases, 3 empty states, North Star + guardrail metrics, and 5 identified risks.
  • Clickable interactive prototype with Home tabs, dot indicators for new clips, scannable flashcards, and an export sheet with instant undo.

Fintech · UPI Payments & Budgeting

CRED Money: Surfacing Spend Insights at the Exact Moment of Attention

Teardown User Research RICE Scoring PRD

CRED Money already generated powerful budgeting analytics, but engagement was low. Reframing the challenge from an "information deficit" to an "invisibility problem" shifted the intervention from a buried dashboard to an inline trigger.

Discovery & Evaluation

  • Complete product teardown of CRED’s UPI journey and merchant settlement touchpoints.
  • Conducted user interviews and a 15-participant quantitative survey to pinpoint drop-off moments.
  • Pivoted prioritization from ICE to RICE after discovering reach estimates obscured core habit loops.

The Solution & Tradeoffs

  • Engineered an inline category spend-bar showing live budget velocity directly on the payment success screen.
  • Deliberate UX tradeoff: Placed strictly post-payment to preserve frictionless instant checkout while leveraging high-intent attention.
  • Delivered a production-ready P0 PRD with error states and fallback handling.
